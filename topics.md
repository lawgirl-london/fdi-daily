# FDI Daily – editorial brief

Edit this file to steer the briefing. Changes apply from the next episode.

## Listener
London-based lawyer, expert in FDI screening / national-security investment control.
Assume deep expertise: no basics, correct terminology, name the instrument, authority,
sector, parties and dates.

## Priority 1 – FDI screening regimes (core of every episode)
- **UK** – National Security and Investment Act 2021: Investment Security Unit (Cabinet Office),
  call-in notices, final orders, notification statistics, annual report, changes to the
  17 mandatory sectors / Notifiable Acquisition Regulations, guidance, judicial review.
  Adjacent: CMA public-interest interventions, Enterprise Act PIIs.
- **EU** – FDI Screening Regulation (2019/452) and its revision; cooperation mechanism;
  Commission annual report; Foreign Subsidies Regulation (in-depth reviews, decisions);
  outbound investment monitoring; economic security agenda.
- **EU member states** – Germany (AWG/AWV, Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs, planned
  Investment Screening Act), France (Code monétaire et financier, Bercy), Italy (Golden Power),
  Spain, Netherlands (Vifo Act), Austria, Poland, Sweden, Denmark, Czechia, Belgium, Ireland.
- **US** – CFIUS (decisions, mitigation, penalties, rules, annual report, real-estate
  jurisdiction, Known Investor / fast-track initiatives), outbound investment regime
  (Treasury), America First Investment Policy, Team Telecom, state foreign-land-ownership laws.
- **Rest of world** – Canada (Investment Canada Act), Australia (FIRB), New Zealand,
  Japan (FEFTA), South Korea, China (security review, countermeasures), India (Press Note 3),
  Gulf states, Switzerland (new investment screening law).

## Priority 1b – deals of interest (any country with an FDI regime)
Transactions where an FDI / national-security screening authority has:
- **blocked or prohibited** the deal, or ordered a divestment / unwinding;
- cleared it **subject to conditions or mitigation** (final orders, mitigation agreements,
  golden-power prescriptions, undertakings, conditional approvals);
- caused it to be **abandoned or withdrawn** because of screening concerns;
- **called it in / opened an in-depth review** that is publicly known (tracked as pending,
  so the outcome can be reported later).

Where to look: gov.uk NSI Act final-order notices, Italian golden-power decrees (Palazzo
Chigi), German and French press on ministry decisions, CFIUS-related filings and company
announcements, Australian Treasurer statements, Investment Canada Act orders, plus Reuters,
FT, Bloomberg, MLex and law-firm deal trackers. Many regimes don't publish decisions, so
reliable press reporting counts – attribute it.

## Priority 2 – closely related
- Export controls and sanctions where they intersect with inbound/outbound investment.
- Policy papers and statistics: OECD, UNCTAD Investment Policy Monitor, World Investment Report.

## Priority 3 – wider world (short segment, 1–2 minutes)
Global headlines that matter for cross-border investment: geopolitics, trade measures,
major cross-border M&A, sanctions, economic security.

## Preferred sources
Primary: gov.uk (NSI Act notices, ISU), Official Journal of the EU, European Commission press
corner, BMWE, Légifrance, US Treasury (CFIUS), Federal Register, FIRB, Investment Canada.
Secondary: Reuters, Financial Times, Bloomberg, Politico Europe, MLex, Law360, Global Trade
Review, law-firm FDI briefings and trackers.
